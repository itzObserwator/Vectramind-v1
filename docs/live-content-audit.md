The comparison covers 28 published endpoints: nine main pages, nine Firstpass module destinations and ten articles. Repeated navigation, repeated enquiry forms and repeated Firstpass suite introductions are consolidated into shared components. Existing designs, logo artwork, animation pacing and requested mobile exclusions are retained.

## Editorial handling

- Business copy is rewritten for clarity and grouped by what visitors need to understand; repeated slogans and duplicated page furniture are consolidated.
- The Synapse headline's **2.8 billion** and the shared **2B+** reach statistic both appear on the live site. The detailed product page uses 2.8B; the existing shared reach label remains 2B+.
- Gallery captions are checked against visible information: one image labelled “Saha 2024” by the live HTML is visibly a **20th anniversary graphic dated 10 June 2026**. It is captioned accordingly; mixed team photos use “Saha & team moments” without an unverified event year. Original source labels remain in the gallery metadata. The 18+ company-years figure remains a published lower bound, rather than an inferred founding date.
- Company experience (**18+ years**) and the culture page's **50+ years of industry expertise** retain their different scopes. No founding date or certification is inferred.
- Uncited sector statistics on the scheduler page (45% / 20-minute frustration) and consent page (over 50% of a doctor's day entering EHR data) are not promoted as new verified facts. Their underlying themes—waiting-time friction and administrative workload—are retained. No underlying study is identified by those source pages.
- The livecast learning period is attributed as a published module description, with data/deployment dependence made clear. Clinical, financial or satisfaction benefits are described as supported goals, rather than guaranteed results.
- Current mobile exclusions for Careers, Contact, Insights and the client directory are preserved. The smaller static major-brand strip remains available on mobile.

## Main implementation locations

- `src/content/products.ts`, `synapse-details.ts`, `firstpass-modules.ts`, `capabilities.ts`, `global-reach.ts`, `experience.ts`.
- `src/components/pages/firstpass-module-page.tsx` and `/firstpass/[module]` provide the nine new static routes.
- Product, About, Careers and shared enquiry components hold the corresponding expanded sections.
- Full Firstpass module descriptions stay in server-rendered pages; the interactive overview tabs only contain lightweight summaries and route links.
